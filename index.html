n8n Gmail Automation

A private automation used by its owner to prepare email replies and support personal productivity workflows.

The Gmail drafting workflow reads incoming messages and relevant conversation context, checks whether the sender matches an existing HubSpot contact, and uses OpenAI Codex to prepare a reply. Supported attachments may be processed when needed for the draft. Replies are saved as unsent drafts in Gmail for the owner to review and send manually.

This is a personal tool, not a public service. There is no public registration or access to the owner's mailbox.

Related personal briefing workflows use connected calendar and task information to prepare daily summaries.
