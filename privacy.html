n8n Gmail Automation — Privacy Policy

Purpose and users

n8n Gmail Automation is operated by its owner for personal email drafting and related productivity workflows. It is not offered as a public service.

Information processed

The email workflow processes message text, subject lines, sender and recipient details, message and thread identifiers, relevant conversation history, and supported attachments. It checks sender email addresses against the owner's existing HubSpot contacts. Related briefing workflows process authorized calendar information and the owner's task information.

How information is used

Information is used to prepare relevant email replies, avoid duplicate drafts, track processing status, and produce personal summaries. Email replies are created as unsent Gmail drafts for the owner to review. The drafting workflow does not automatically send email.

Services involved

The automation runs through a local n8n instance and local bridge services. Relevant email content and supported attachment content are provided to OpenAI through Codex for draft generation. Sender email addresses are used for HubSpot contact lookups. Gmail stores the resulting drafts. Related briefing workflows use Supabase for personal tasks and summaries; earlier briefing files may remain in Google Drive.

Running the automation locally does not mean all processing stays on the computer. Connected services process the information required for their part of the workflow under the owner's account settings and their applicable terms and policies.

Storage and access

The local installation stores workflow configuration, authorization information, and processing records. Messages and drafts remain in Gmail until managed by the owner. Related tasks, summaries, and existing files remain in their connected services until managed by the owner. Local temporary files and service-side retention depend on the relevant component and account settings; this notice does not promise immediate deletion or a fixed retention period.

Stopping access and managing information

The owner can stop the workflows and revoke the application's Google access through Google Account settings. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not itself delete existing drafts, summaries, or local records. Those records must be managed separately in their respective locations.

Contact

Use the support email displayed on this application's Google consent screen to contact the operator.
